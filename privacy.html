Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

LifeOS is a personal tool used only by its owner. It requests read-only access to the owner's own sleep data through Google APIs.

What data is used

Sleep sessions and sleep duration from the signed-in Google account. No other data is requested.

How it is used

The data is used only to display the owner's sleep numbers in their private notes workspace. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any automated decision.

Storage and sharing

Data and access tokens are stored only on computers controlled by the owner. They are never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LifeOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
